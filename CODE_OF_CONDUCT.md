# Code of conduct

Repro follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is
expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work,
not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are
not accepted.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through this repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/DuhItzAniket/Repro/security/advisories/new) (the
"Security" tab, then "Report a vulnerability"), and start its title with "Conduct:". It is the
same private channel [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) uses; only the maintainer sees it. Reports are
kept confidential, and we aim to answer within 7 days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response in line with the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban,
depending on what happened. Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this
code may be edited or removed.
